# Mianzhu Woodblock New Year Picture Preset

Use only when the user explicitly selects **Mianzhu woodblock New Year picture**. This is a locked Chinese traditional New Year-picture rendering component—not a generic Chinese-New-Year filter, a red-and-gold article treatment, or shorthand for every regional year-picture tradition.

```text

```

Lock silhouette, proportional/face treatment, signature clothing and props, printed line-block contour, palette roles and placement, hand-painted-color behavior, and composition grammar in `visual-spec.md`; repeat in `prompt-pack.md`; use an accepted visual master as downstream identity reference.

## Craft and Visual Core

Mianzhu uses a **printed deep-ink line-block contour, then hand-painted color**. The line block gives order; color retains a visible, slightly variable human hand. It is festive and decorative, but must keep a clear active subject and legible action.

- Ancient, fluent, firm deep-ink contours; never dense pen hatching, brushy ink wash, or rough woodcut scratches.
- Hand-painted opaque-to-semi-opaque folk color over/within the printed contour; clear color blocks with small painted variation, never vector-flat fills, airbrush modeling, plastic gradients, or blanket metallic gold.
- Complete, balanced, lively composition with dominant subject, primary/secondary hierarchy, and only semantically necessary motifs.
- Lightly idealized, warm human figures with stable IP recognition; never chibi, generic ancient beauty, opera-costume cosplay, or static poster mascot.
- Bright simple contrast that remains harmonious. Gold is optional and only a small local auspicious focal accent.

## Required Palette Roles

Define and preserve: warm paper neutral; main subject/garment color; secondary support color; deep line anchor; auspicious warm accent (vermilion or brick red); cool balance (mineral green or blue-green); optional restrained gold focal accent; natural skin range; subdued environment supports.

Saturation belongs mainly to central action. Backgrounds and secondary motifs cannot compete with it.

## Regional and Style Boundaries

- **Yangliuqing**: more refined, half-print/half-paint, gongbi-influenced finish.
- **Taohuawu**: finer line/space, dense shading and elegant Jiangnan print language.
- **Yangjiabu**: more rustic, strongly exaggerated folk language.
- **Zhuxianzhen**: forceful, frequently symmetric guardian/poster language.

Do not merge these with Mianzhu. Also exclude Chinese lianhuanhua, gongbi line-and-color, freehand ink wash, generic guochao, red-gold commercial festival posters, vector papercut, manga/comics, glossy digital illustration, 3D, and photorealism unless the whole IP is revised.

## Best-Fit Use

Use for an instantly memorable celebration, welcome, renewal, promise, milestone, shared value, or auspicious transformation. For technical subjects, translate only the high-level proposition or turning point into one tangible human-scale symbolic action. Do not use it for dense system diagrams, tiny UI logic, or precise component anatomy.

The IP must cause the narrative change—organize, present, transform, open, welcome, connect—not merely stand inside ornament. Do not fill every empty area just because the composition is complete.

## QA

Accept only when:

- recognition survives through silhouette, signature props/clothing, printed-contour treatment, and fixed palette placement;
- the image clearly reads as print-and-paint craft: line-block structure plus hand-painted color, not generic New-Year poster/festival branding;
- color is bright but role-based, harmonious, and focal—not neon, blanket red-gold, vector-flat, or metallic luxury styling;
- contours are fluent and structurally clear—not gongbi micro-detail, woodcut texture, hatching, or ink-wash blur;
- composition is complete, balanced, and hierarchical; support motifs do not crowd out the IP action;
- gold, if used, stays local and restrained;
- figures, hands, props, and action are readable at article size; removing the IP weakens the cognitive anchor.

Reject/revise if it becomes an e-commerce banner, generic guochao collage, papercut vector art, static guardian pose, another regional year-picture tradition, or ornament overwhelms the action.
