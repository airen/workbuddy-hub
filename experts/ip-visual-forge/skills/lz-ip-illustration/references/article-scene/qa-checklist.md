# QA Checklist

Run this before finalizing a prompt or generated image.

For article covers, use `references/cover/cover-qa-checklist.md` in addition to identity checks below.
For Xiaohongshu/Green Book card series, use `references/xhs-card/xhs-card-qa-checklist.md` in addition to identity checks below.

## Identity Stability

- Same character species/person/object as the IP profile.
- Same body proportion and signature features.
- Same outfit or clearly allowed variation.
- Same palette and rendering style.
- No accidental age, species, gender, face, or style drift.
- If the rendering style is black-and-white, the IP remains recognizable through silhouette, clothing/prop shape, and defined light-midtone-dark anchors rather than hue alone.

## Scene Participation

- IP performs a necessary action.
- Single-scene illustration has one primary IP action only.
- Secondary gestures support the primary action and do not create another explanatory event.
- If multiple actions are needed, the plan uses multiple images or mini-comic panels.
- Removing the IP would damage the explanation.
- Action is connected to the article's cognitive anchor.
- IP is not just holding a sign, standing aside, or acting as a watermark.

## Content Clarity

- Image explains one idea, not a whole article.
- Image explains one core structure, not a whole article.
- Composition pattern matches the content type.
- Labels are short and readable.
- Visual objects are not random decoration.
- The scene can be understood at article-reading size.

## Reuse Quality

- Fits the existing IP visual system.
- Can sit next to prior images as part of one series.
- Avoids over-specific one-off details unless the article requires them.
- Does not create a new style direction by accident.

## Text and Language

- Text language matches the article language.
- Chinese article illustrations use short Chinese labels or no text.
- No accidental English paragraphs, fake UI copy, or dense whiteboard text unless requested.
- In-image text is large enough to read at article size.
- No local text overlay/compositing was used.
- Required labels are integrated into the scene, usually 5-10 Chinese characters.
- Chinese text is not garbled, deformed, misspelled, visually detached, or covering the IP action.

## Traditional Chinese Baimiao Realistic Lianhuanhua (when selected)

- Fine ink contours, hatching, and cross-hatching remain legible at intended reading size.
- Anatomy, hands, clothing folds, and scene depth are coherent and restrainedly realistic.
- The result does not drift into Japanese manga/anime, American comics, woodcut, freehand ink-wash, colorful gongbi, propaganda poster, glossy digital painting, or generic retro filter imagery.
- Paper texture supports the image without obscuring the IP action or article cognitive anchor.

## Pen-and-Ink Realistic Lianhuanhua (when selected)

- Crisp pen contours, hatching, cross-hatching, and selective stippling remain legible at intended reading size.
- Tonal form comes from deliberate pen-line construction and white-paper reserve, not smooth digital grayscale or indiscriminate dark fill.
- The result reads as pen-and-ink realistic lianhuanhua, not Traditional Chinese baimiao, Japanese manga/anime, American comics, woodcut, freehand ink-wash, colorful gongbi, propaganda poster, glossy digital painting, or generic retro filter imagery.
- Anatomy, hands, clothing folds, perspective, and spatial depth are coherent; tonal density does not obscure the IP action or article cognitive anchor.

## Gongbi Line-and-Color Realistic Lianhuanhua (when selected)

- Fine ink outlines remain visible and clearly contain the layered color fields at intended reading size.
- Palette roles and signature color placement remain stable across the IP visual system; color supports hierarchy rather than becoming uniformly saturated decoration.
- The result reads as gongbi line-and-color realistic lianhuanhua, not generic Chinese-fantasy digital art, freehand ink-wash, black-and-white baimiao, pen-and-ink hatching-only drawing, woodcut, Japanese manga/anime, poster art, or glossy game concept art.
- Anatomy, hands, clothing folds, perspective, and spatial depth are coherent; glossy gradients, heavy gold/red ornament, or excessive surface detail do not obscure the IP action or article cognitive anchor.

## Woodcut-Print Realistic Lianhuanhua (when selected)

- The IP remains recognizable through silhouette, stance, signature prop shape, and stable black-white mass placement rather than tiny details or print noise.
- Carved knife-cut marks and limited carved midtones follow form, light, material, or movement; they do not become random scratches, evenly striped texture, smooth grayscale, or unreadable heavy fill.
- The result reads as realistic woodcut-print lianhuanhua, not a propaganda poster, generic folk-art stamp, pen-and-ink drawing, baimiao, gongbi color art, freehand ink-wash, Japanese manga/anime, or glossy digital illustration.
- Black-white contrast preserves focal hierarchy and article-size readability for face, hands, tools, and the central action; heroic-poster poses, political symbols, decorative patterns, or excessive grain do not obscure the cognitive anchor.

## Late-20th-Century Chinese Realistic Watercolor-and-Gouache Textbook Illustration (when selected)

- The IP remains recognizable through stable silhouette, approachable expression range, clothing/prop shape, and consistent low-saturation palette placement.
- The result reads as a hand-painted late-20th-century Chinese realistic textbook illustration, not Chinese lianhuanhua, gongbi, ink wash, generic children’s decorative cartoon, glossy digital art, manga, or photo realism.
- Watercolor washes and selective matte gouache accents remain visible and controlled; they do not become muddy airbrush gradients, a generic retro filter, heavy paper texture, or uniformly opaque flat color.
- Gentle natural light, open composition, readable faces/hands, lived environmental context, and article-size clarity support the explanatory action; nostalgia, decorative period props, or sentiment do not replace it.

## Mianzhu Woodblock New Year Picture (when selected)

- The IP remains recognizable through stable silhouette, clothing/prop shapes, fixed palette placement, and the same deep-ink printed-contour plus hand-painted-color relationship.
- The result reads as Mianzhu print-and-paint craft: fluent, firm line-block contours followed by visibly hand-painted folk color—not a generic red-and-gold New Year advertisement, generic guochao, papercut vector, or undifferentiated folk image.
- Bright color remains role-based, harmonious, and concentrated on the subject/action; it does not become blanket red-gold saturation, neon, perfectly flat vector fill, plastic gradient, or glossy metallic luxury styling.
- Contours are ancient, fluent, and structurally clear rather than gongbi micro-detail, woodcut scratches, dense pen hatching, or freehand ink-wash blur.
- Composition is complete, balanced, and hierarchical. Support motifs are semantically necessary and do not crowd out the active IP or force irrelevant symmetry.
- Gold, if used, is a single restrained local auspicious emphasis, never page-wide glitter. The scene does not drift into Yangliuqing, Taohuawu, Yangjiabu, or Zhuxianzhen conventions.
- Figures, hands, props, and the central action remain readable at article size; the IP causes the welcome, renewal, milestone, or transformation rather than posing as a removable festive ornament.

## Chinese Shadow Puppetry (when selected)

- The IP remains recognisable through stable side-profile or controlled three-quarter silhouette, articulated-joint grammar, costume/prop shape, pierced-cutout motif family, palette placement, and the same screen/backlight relationship.
- The result reads as Chinese shadow-puppetry craft and performance: carved translucent figure plus warm transmitted light through a screen--not generic paper-cut, flat red silhouette, opaque vector icon, generic guochao, or New-Year poster.
- Carved perimeter, restrained pierced details, joints, hands, props, and the action/result remain readable at article size; decorative perforation does not overwhelm the cognitive anchor.
- Translucent color is role-based and visibly backlit, not blanket red-gold saturation, neon, metallic luxury styling, plastic gradient, or photorealistic studio lighting.
- Composition is shallow, staged, and hierarchical. Rods, joint angles, hands, gaze, prop direction, and the light path clarify the IP-caused action rather than serving as ornamental theatre dressing.
- The image does not falsely attribute a regional shadow-puppetry tradition without explicit, verified constraints, and does not drift into a deep-perspective theatre scene, costume collage, or unjointed decorative puppet.

## Hand-Drawn Sketchnote Technical Draft (when selected)

- The IP remains recognisable through silhouette, clothing/prop shapes, and repeated action vocabulary, not through tiny labels or one-off decorative marks.
- The result reads as an intentional hand-drawn sketchnote technical draft: white/light paper ground, black-gray loose-but-purposeful linework, visible reasoning traces, restrained accent annotations, and clear whitespace.
- Layout supports one explainable idea through a compact diagram, comparison, route, or note cluster; it does not become a dense whiteboard screenshot or a full article squeezed into one image.
- In-image text, if used, is limited to short readable labels; no fake UI paragraphs, unreadable microcopy, or English filler in Chinese article illustrations.
- Draft texture stays controlled: no messy doodling, generic retro paper filter, polished vector infographic, glossy digital illustration, manga/anime, or pen-and-ink lianhuanhua drift.
- The IP actively performs the reasoning action--sorting, connecting, diagnosing, marking, comparing, or repairing--instead of standing beside a finished poster.

## Visual Master Specific

- Character is not cropped.
- Full body or clear half-body identity is visible.
- Signature outfit, color, body proportion, and face are easy to reuse.
- Supporting props explain the domain without overpowering the character.
- Avoid locking accidental details that should remain flexible, such as exact whiteboard text or one-off badge wording.
